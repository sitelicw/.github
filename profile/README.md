# SITELI CW — Circular Water

**Mobile industrial water reuse, built in Morocco.**

We build modular, mobile water treatment units that help industrial sites recycle their
wastewater, cut freshwater consumption, and bring treatment capacity onto a site quickly —
without new construction.

> *"Wastewater is not a problem. It's a resource in the wrong place."*

---

## The problem we work on

Most industrial sites run water in a straight line. Fresh water is drawn in and paid for in
full, put to work in cooling towers, washdown and process loops, then treated just enough to
meet discharge regulations and released. The treatment step is a cost centre built for
compliance, not for recovery — so every litre gets bought once and thrown away once.

The unit closes that line. The process itself doesn't change; what changes is where the water
goes when it leaves it.

## How the system works

The skid arrives sized to the site's flow rate and water chemistry, and connects into existing
plumbing rather than requiring new construction.

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Wastewater in** | The stream is characterised before anything is configured |
| **Filtration** | Solids come out |
| **UV disinfection** | The water is brought up to spec |
| **Advanced treatment** | Applied as the stream's chemistry requires — not every unit needs it |
| **Reuse + monitoring** | Treated water goes back to work; performance is tracked |

The unit is mobile by design, so it can serve as a permanent fixture or relocate as an
operation's needs change.

## What we're targeting

These are targets and design capabilities, not guarantees. Actual results depend on the
stream, the treatment configuration, and site conditions — assessed case by case.

- **80–95% water reuse** — referenced under optimised conditions
- **Deployment in hours, not months** — typical, and still dependent on site prep, permitting
  and water analysis
- **24/7 monitoring** — a design target for the platform as it rolls out, not a claim about
  every unit today

## HydroMind++

The digital layer, currently rolling out. It is intended to surface flow rate, pressure and
treatment performance as the system runs; water quality and reuse performance; system status
and alerts; and operating parameters logged for review over time.

**The software layer is intended to be open source**, so operators' own teams can read it,
audit it and deploy it themselves rather than trust a black box. It is designed to run hosted
by us, entirely inside a customer's own environment, or split between the two.
